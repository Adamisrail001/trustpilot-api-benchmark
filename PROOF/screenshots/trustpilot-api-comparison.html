Reading this chart
  • There's no weighted "overall score" here. lobstr.io was the only provider that could complete a full review-collection job (2,000/2,000 in a separate scalability test, 5,000/5,000 in a separate 5-business multi-task test), so scoring it against tools capped at 200 reviews per business on speed, cost, or scale wouldn't be an apples-to-apples comparison.
  • The 5,000-review figure is from a distinct live test (2026-09-04): 5 businesses added as tasks to one squid, one run, target raised to 1,000/business. Raw evidence: data/lobstr/five-domain-multi-task-test-2026-09-04/ in the repo linked below.
  • Apify's data quality isn't a single number — a different business returned 0 reviews while still reporting SUCCEEDED in each of 3 live runs. Field coverage above reflects only the runs where the ground-truth business succeeded.
  • The hatched Apify bar in "Total reviews, 5 businesses, one run" is a different Actor (memo23/trustpilot-scraper-ppe, not automation-lab/trustpilot), tested on 2026-09-16 on an upgraded, paid Apify account. An initial test used 5 separate Actor runs out of caution (this Actor's multi-URL behavior was unconfirmed at the time); a same-day follow-up confirmed it also works in a single run with all 5 businesses at once — 182s, faster than lobstr.io's 398.6s for the identical volume. The same Actor returned only 50/1,000 on a free account 13 days earlier — it is not the Actor scored anywhere else on this page. Raw evidence: outputs/apify-alt-actor-five-domain-bulk-test/ and outputs/apify-alt-actor-single-run-multi-domain-test/ (gitignored; summarized in knowledge.md).
  • Outscraper and OpenWeb Ninja were also tested but aren't charted here — see the article's FAQ for how they performed.
  • Full methodology and raw evidence: github.com/Adamisrail001/trustpilot-api-benchmark
