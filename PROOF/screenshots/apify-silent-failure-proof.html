Why this matters: if you only check run.status === "SUCCEEDED", you'll ship a pipeline that silently drops an entire business's reviews with no exception thrown, no retry triggered, and no field in the response flagging it. The only way to catch it is an independent row-count check per business against what you requested — the platform's own success signal doesn't do it for you.
